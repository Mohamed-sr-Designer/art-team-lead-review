How I will set priorities for the team

Priorities are currently set per request, which makes everything look urgent. From Week 2 they are set per week, using the same four steps, so capacity decisions are made with data rather than in the moment.

Step 01See the capacityList the work we promised against the design days we actually have that week.
Step 02Ask three questionsIs the date real? What breaks if it moves? Can we cut scope instead of the date?
Step 03Set a limitEach designer works on a set number of items at a time. Nothing new starts until something finishes.
Step 04Escalate, do not absorbOver capacity goes to you as a choice: cut scope, move the date, or add people. Not into my evenings.

Part 3 / 03

A plan for each person, not one plan for everyone.

My team is not at one level, so one plan for all of them does not work. I split them by how ready each person is to own work today. This is how initiative 04 gets executed: capability spread across the team rather than concentrated in a few people.

How I grouped them: by how ready each person is to own an account and carry the decisions that come with it today. It is not about talent or effort, and it is not permanent. We score it together in Week 3, and I expect people to move from Group B to Group A inside the 60 days. That movement is one of the things we measure.
Group A · Ready to own

Turn them into owners

Goal: people who own projects and need very little supervision from me.

Find the blocker, then remove it

Goal: understand what is actually stopping each person, then fix it. Most of what is missing here is support I did not give.

HHeba Senior AlAlice Video ShShaimaa Video
Action plan
  • One to one every two weeks, with a written follow up each time.
  • Find the real gap for each person: a skill, unclear expectations, or something I am not giving them.
  • Agree one personal development goal each, in their words not mine.
  • Bring in new ways of working slowly, one at a time, not four at once.
  • Give feedback continuously and follow up on it, instead of saving it for a review.
  • Pair them with a Group A designer where it helps.
Expected outcome
  • Better engagement, because the goal is agreed and not pushed.
  • Higher use of the new workflow, because it arrives at a speed people can absorb.
  • More confidence, because feedback comes early and often.
  • More consistent creative quality across the whole team.

My commitment · 05

I accept the review. The score shows a real gap between what this job needs and how I worked in my first 90 days. I worked on the output because output was the thing I could control and show quickly. That gave good work and a team that is not independent yet, and the second one matters more to this company.

This plan is written so you can check it, not so you can believe it. Every promise has a date, an owner and a number. Where there is no starting number, I have said so, and I will measure before I claim anything.

I am asking you for two things: keep the day 30 and day 60 checks in the calendar, and give me a decision when I bring you a scope choice, so that too much work becomes a business decision instead of my own overtime.

If the numbers do not move by day 60, that is also a result. I will bring it to you with the same detail as if they had.
